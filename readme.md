## About

Northstar is a laptop shop concept for people who want practical, thoughtful advice instead of an overwhelming wall of specifications. The page highlights three sample laptop picks, explains the shop's approach, and includes a contact form layout.

## Design System

### Palette (60-30-10)

| Role | Share | Color | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dominant | 60% | `#f5f3ed` | Page background |
| Secondary | 30% | `#fffefa` | Product cards and form surfaces |
| Accent | 10% | `#244b3e` and `#d7ed76` | Buttons, links, highlights |

### Visual style

**Style chosen:** Warm editorial minimalism.

**Why I chose it:** Calm colors, expressive type, and generous space make choosing a laptop feel less overwhelming.

### Contrast check

Body text color vs. background color: `#17231e` on `#f5f3ed`

Contrast ratio: 14.3 : 1
